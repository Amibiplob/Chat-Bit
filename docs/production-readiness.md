# Production readiness

Current target: one Neon-only Next.js PWA on Vercel, custom authentication, private
attachments and WebRTC. Follow deployment.md and environment.md for fresh setup.
Old-data copying, backups and Android packaging are intentionally excluded.

Local automated evidence is recorded here after the final verification milestone.
A passing local suite is not proof of live Neon database/storage access, production
authentication, real-device installation or cross-network TURN calling. The live
database is never initialized without explicit approval.
